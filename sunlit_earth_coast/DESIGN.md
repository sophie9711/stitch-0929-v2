## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the sensory abundance, longevity, and vitality of Mediterranean coastal living. It translates fresh, unprocessed culinary traditions into a luminous digital sanctuary. The design movement pairs **Organic Contemporary Minimalism** with **Refined Tactile Editorial Warmth**, evoking hand-pressed olive oil bottles, artisanal ceramic earthenware, sun-bleached coastal stonework, and hand-lettered gastronomy journals.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tone
- **Sensory & Sun-Drenched:** Generous airy layouts that let vibrant food imagery breathe alongside tactile warm neutrals.
- **Nourishing & Unhurried:** Interaction flows that prioritize mindful meal preparation, seasonal abundance, and clarity over frantic calorie-tracking metrics.
- **Gastronomic Precision:** Editorial typography paired with structured nutritional badges ensures authoritative guidance without medicinal sterility.

### Target Audience
Health-conscious epicures, home cooks, and individuals adopting cardiac-healthy lifestyles who value flavor, fresh whole foods, and a tactile, elevated lifestyle experience over clinical diet apps.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the topography and botanicals of Mediterranean groves, terracotta rooftops, and coastal tides. The default color mode is strictly `light`, establishing a sunlit, natural paper canvas.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#2D5A27` — Deep Olive Grove):** Used for anchor navigation, primary actions, selected states, and authoritative typography. Conveys nourishment and herbal freshness.
- **Secondary (`#D96B43` — Warm Terracotta):** Used for micro-actions, call-outs, flame/heat indicators, savory badges, and heart actions. Evokes sun-baked ceramics and roasted spices.
- **Tertiary (`#2A7F9E` — Aegean Blue):** Dedicated to hydration tracking, seafood classifications, cold prep metrics, and secondary feature discoverability.
- **Neutral Core (`#2C302E` — Natural Charcoal):** High-legibility, soft mineral tone for primary reading copy, preventing the harsh artificial edge of pure `#000000`.
- **Canvas (`#FBF9F5` — Warm Sand):** The primary background canvas, providing an organic, unbleached parchment warmth.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` — Crisp Calcite):** Card layers and modals that sit cleanly on top of the warm sand background.
- **Subtle Structural Accent (`#E3ECE3` — Muted Sage):** Low-contrast dividers, pill container fills, and delicate perimeter borders that soften layout transitions.

## Typography

The typography couples the editorial, artisanal presence of **Epilogue** for display and headline roles with the friendly, geometric, hyper-legible warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for body copy and interface labels.

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Display & Large Headlines (Epilogue):** Set with snug letter spacing (`-0.02em`) to maintain editorial impact. Used for meal hero titles, recipe collection covers, and dashboard date milestones.
- **Body Text (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Set at regular weight (`400`) with generous line heights to facilitate effortless reading of multi-step recipes and nutritional analyses on mobile devices.
- **Labels, Badges, & Metrics (Plus Jakarta Sans Semi-Bold):** Set in medium to semi-bold weights (`600`) with neutral tracking for macros, prep timings, and pantry checklist items.

## Layout & Spacing

This mobile-first system implements a fluid, breathable spatial structure optimized for single-thumb navigation while preserving editorial breathing room.

### Spatial System & Breakpoints
- **Mobile Handset (320px - 480px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` (`20px`) canvas margin and `1rem` (`16px`) gutter. Components flow vertically; carousels utilize horizontal snap-scrolling bleeding 20px off canvas.
- **Tablet / Large Handset (481px - 840px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` (`32px`) canvas margin. Cards reflow into 2-column masonry or balanced grids.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Element stacking respects a strict 4px base scale: micro-spacers use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px); card insets and component separations use `space-md` (16px), `space-lg` (24px), and `space-xl` (32px).

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system rejects aggressive dropshadows and heavy industrial borders. Depth is realized through **sun-drenched atmospheric layering**:

### Elevation Planes
1. **Ground Canvas (Sand `#FBF9F5`):** Base layer housing underlying page scrolls, category scroll chips, and structural headers.
2. **Elevated Surface (Crisp White `#FFFFFF`):** Primary recipe cards, ingredient lists, and meal log panels. Uses a soft ambient tinted shadow: `0px 4px 16px rgba(44, 48, 46, 0.04), 0px 1px 3px rgba(44, 48, 46, 0.02)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Menus:** Modal sheets, bottom navigation bar, and floating recipe action bars. Casts an extended coastal shadow: `0px 12px 32px rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.08), 0px 2px 6px rgba(44, 48, 46, 0.03)`.
4. **Border Accents:** Cards and inputs feature a whisper-thin outline: `1px solid #E3ECE3`, yielding tactile structure without visual weight.

## Shapes

The geometric vernacular is organic, soft, and approachable, avoiding severe razor-sharp corners in favor of generous radii reminiscent of river stones, olive oil droplets, and artisanal stoneware.

### Shape Scales
- **Standard Surfaces (Cards, Panels):** `1rem` to `1.5rem` (`16px` - `24px`) radii, providing welcoming contours on touchscreens.
- **Form Controls & Inputs:** `0.75rem` (`12px`) for field boxes; fully circular (`9999px`) for pill buttons, search fields, and metric badges.
- **Media Containers:** Recipe thumbnails feature asymmetrical or softly rounded corners (`1.25rem` / `20px`) to highlight food textures naturally.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Olive (`#2D5A27`) background with Crisp White text, pill-shaped (`9999px`), `space-md` horizontal padding, height `48px`. Active state subtly dims with an inner tint.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent fill with `1.5px` border in Terracotta (`#D96B43`), matching text color, pill-shaped.
- **Tertiary Ghost / Text Action:** Natural Charcoal or Deep Olive with no border, `label-md` styling, accompanied by subtle directional micro-icons.

### Recipe & Nutrition Cards
- **Base Card:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) surface, `20px` corner radius, `1px` border in Muted Sage (`#E3ECE3`), ambient micro-shadow.
- **Header:** Full-bleed or inset imagery with an embedded Terracotta bookmark/favorite icon in the top right.
- **Footer:** Inline row featuring Prep Time badge, Caloric/Macro breakdown, and heart-healthy dietary indicators.

### Badges & Nutrition Chips
- **Time & Prep Badges:** Warm Sand background (`#FBF9F5`) with Deep Olive text and a micro-clock glyph.
- **Omega-3 & Hearth Indicators:** Pale Aegean Blue tint (`rgba(42, 127, 158, 0.12)`) with Aegean Blue text (`#2A7F9E`).
- **Antioxidant & Harvest Highlights:** Pale Terracotta tint (`rgba(217, 107, 67, 0.12)`) with Terracotta text (`#D96B43`).
- All badges are fully pill-shaped (`9999px`) with `space-xs` vertical and `space-sm` horizontal padding.

### Lists & Ingredient Checklists
- **Ingredient Rows:** Crisp White rows divided by hairline `1px` `#E3ECE3` dividers. Left-aligned custom organic checkbox; right-aligned volumetric quantities in Natural Charcoal (`body-md` semi-bold).
- **Checkboxes:** `20px` soft-rounded squares (`rounded-md`, 6px radius) with `#E3ECE3` border when unchecked. When checked, transitions to a Deep Olive fill with a crisp white check icon and strike-through on ingredient text.

### Form Inputs & Search
- **Search Bar:** Pill-shaped (`9999px`), Warm Sand (`#FBF9F5`) fill with a `1px` Muted Sage border. Inset magnifying lens icon in Olive Green.
- **Text Inputs:** White background, `12px` border radius, `1px` `#E3ECE3` border. On focus, transitions to `#2D5A27` border with a subtle 2px soft green ring (`rgba(45, 90, 39, 0.15)`).

### Navigation & Tab Bar
- **Floating Island Navigation:** Anchored `16px` above screen bottom, Crisp White background with `28px` corner radius, protected by high-radius ambient shadow.
- **Tab Items:** 4 primary destinations (Today, Discover, Pantry, Profile). Active tab displays Deep Olive filled glyph with a subtle under-dot indicator; inactive tabs display delicate Natural Charcoal icon outlines at 50% opacity.
